**A non-linear model of the relationship between product complexity, institutional transparency, and the accumulation of stakeholder trust over time.**

Trust does not scale linearly with quality. It scales with legibility. In AI markets, complexity is the primary trust destroyer — unless matched by transparency architecture that converts complexity into Complexity Currency: a tradeable unit of institutional credibility.
