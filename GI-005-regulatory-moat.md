# GI-005 · Regulatory Moat
## Beyond AI Disruption — Framework Registry

**The durable competitive advantage created when a firm's proactive compliance architecture exceeds minimum regulatory standards — to the degree that it becomes a market entry barrier.**

A Regulatory Moat is not a defensive posture. It is an offensive one. The moat is the gap between a firm's governance architecture and the competition's capacity to replicate it.
