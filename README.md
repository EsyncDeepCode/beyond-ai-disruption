## The Book

**Beyond AI Disruption** examines why the dominant competitive advantage in AI markets is not disruption itself — but the institutional capacity to contain, govern, and stabilise it.

The book develops six original frameworks across nine chapters, grounded in game theory, institutional economics, and psychometric trust modelling.

## The Living Document Specification

This book is the first published work structured under **The Living Document Specification (v1.0)** — an open-source publishing architecture pioneered by Mohammad EL-Dharrat.

The specification mandates four explicit layers per chapter:

- **Value Layer** — High-signal summary for the time-pressured reader
- **Deep Dive Layer** — Full analytical and evidential substrate
- **Business Hook Layer** — Diagnostic, checklist, or implementable framework
- **Metadata Layer** — Structured semantic block for LLM and PKM ingestion

The structural format is open-sourced under **CC BY 4.0**. You are free to use it for any work, commercial or non-commercial, with attribution.
